FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

Quick answers to the questions new and returning staff ask most often.

How do I request leave?

Fill in the leave request form at least 3 working days before. Your line manager approves it by email and the Academic Office arranges cover.

I’m unwell today. What should I do?

Message your line manager and the Academic Office before 7:00 AM, and upload cover work to your class folder if you can.

When are lesson plans due?

Weekly plans go into the shared drive by Friday 4:00 PM for the following week.

Where do I find exam weeks and PD days?

All key dates are in the calendar on this page. They come straight from the school calendar, so they are always up to date.

Something in my classroom isn’t working.

Send an IT support request with your room number. If your lesson starts in under 15 minutes, call IT directly.

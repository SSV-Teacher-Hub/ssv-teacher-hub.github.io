FAQ

Common questions

How do I request leave?

Fill in the leave request form at least 3 working days before. Your line manager approves it by email and the Academic Office arranges cover.

I’m sick today. What should I do?

Message your line manager and the Academic Office before 7:00 AM, and upload cover work to your class folder if you can.

When are lesson plans due?

Weekly plans go into the shared drive by Friday 4:00 PM for the following week.

Something in my classroom is broken.

Send an IT support request with your room number. If a lesson starts in under 15 minutes, call IT directly.
